# Non-goals

Refusals that keep Spire a game (and Spire AI a category), not a haunted project manager.

## Product

- **Not a Jira skin.** Tickets may link to rooms; rooms are not a backlog dump.
- **Not autopilot PM.** AI weights `?` outcomes; it does not pick the next node for you.
- **Not infinite chat cosplay.** If there’s no map/hand/intent, it’s not the client.
- **Not mandatory gamification.** Points/badges/leaderboards are not the loop.
- **Not “generate the whole app from a boss fight.”** Cards do scoped engineering work; bosses gate decisions and ship readiness.

## Design

- **No multi-room multitasking.** One `active_room` or you’re not playing Spire.
- **No reward dark patterns.** Skip is visible and respected; no fake urgency.
- **No silent ascension.** Difficulty only moves on explicit player action.
- **No engine-stored project secrets.** Save lives in the repo; plugin stays knowledge-free.
- **No model-required core loop.** Sensors + scripts must playable if the curator is absent.

## Content

- **No front-loaded mega decks.** Starters stay small; earn or shop the rest.
- **No invented rooms without a pool id.** Every resolve hits authored content (or Colorless orient).
- **No “just add another always-on rule” as the default card.** Relics are rare; skills are the meat.

## Business

- **No pretending Spire knowledge is required.** Glossary and intent UX teach the ideology in-product.
- **No shipping Stage 5 wrap before Stage 2 is fun.** Entertainment-before-scale.

## When to break a non-goal

Only with a written amendment here + GDD note naming what we give up (focus, agency, lean, or reality bridge).
