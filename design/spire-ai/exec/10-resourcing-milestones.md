# 10 — Resourcing and milestone plan

**Status:** living  
**Owner:** founder  
**Decides:** What to fund next; roles; kill criteria.

## Problem

Design and engine work can fan out across Stages 0–6. Exec focus must freeze **one** active build milestone so content, client, and polish do not starve the loop proof.

## Recommendation

### Active milestone (only)

**Ship-the-stub demo — Stage 2 exit** ([wedge.md](../wedge.md)).

Definition of done (exec):

- Founder dogfood complete on a real stub repo
- **5 external testers** clear + skip without Spire wiki ([metrics](08-success-metrics.md))
- Demo video recordable from the client / play path
- Landing + tester CTA live enough to recruit ([GTM](05-narrative-gtm.md))

Everything else (extra packs, marketplace, enterprise, fundraising model) is **explicitly out of scope** until this gate passes.

### Staffing sketch (even if one founder wears all three)

| Role | Responsibility for Stage 2 |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Game / systems** | Room clear/skip loop, act template, reward feel, kill-criteria judgment |
| **MCP / UI** | Facet chrome for map → intent → combat → reward; wiki-free play |
| **Content authoring** | Ship-the-stub climb copy, starter deck lean-ness, tester onboarding script |

One founder may cover all three; do not hire for marketplace or sales before Stage 2 exit.

### What to fund next (ordered)

1. Stage 2 playable path + tester recruitment
2. Only after gate: first packs/climbs from [07-moat-content](07-moat-content.md)
3. Only after content evidence: paid surface decisions in [04-business-model](04-business-model.md)

### Kill criteria

Stop or hard-reset the product thesis if:

- External testers find the loop **not fun** after one iteration of UX fixes (not “needs more features”)
- Skip/clear feels like **chore bureaucracy** rather than a climb
- Testers require a **wiki** to finish Ship-the-stub after facet fixes
- Active-room discipline is routinely abandoned and players prefer raw agent chat with no Spire chrome

Kill means: pause Stage 3+ spend; rewrite GDD/non-goals or retire the category claim — do not “add Jira sync” as a rescue.
