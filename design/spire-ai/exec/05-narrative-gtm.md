# 05 — Narrative and GTM

**Status:** living  
**Owner:** founder  
**Decides:** Public story spine, Stage 2 launch sequence, channels. Not a full funnel.

## Problem

Spire needs one hero narrative and a minimal launch path so Stage 2 testers and a demo video land before we invent growth loops.

## Recommendation

### Hero narrative

> Coding agents are powerful. What’s missing is the **run**. Spire is a video game for building software with AI: you climb real SDLC work one room at a time, your deck is the agent’s skills and rules, and the save lives in your repo. AI weights the foggy map nodes. You choose the path.

### Three proof points

1. **Real repo save** — `.spire/` + dealt skills/rules; not a separate toy project database.
2. **Clear and skip** — skip is healthy; lean decks beat junk drawers.
3. **Ship-the-stub without a wiki** — five external players finish the demo climb using the client facets alone.

### Stage 2 GTM checklist

Aligned with [wedge](../wedge.md) Stage 2:

- [ ] **Dogfood** — founder clears Ship-the-stub on a real stub repo end-to-end
- [ ] **5 external testers** — primary ICP ([02](02-icp-jtbd.md)); no Spire wiki required
- [ ] **Demo video** — record Ship-the-stub: title → map → room → reward → clear
- [ ] **Landing** — short page: category sentence + video + waitlist / tester CTA
- [ ] **Channels** — Cursor plugin ecosystem; X / LinkedIn; harrisonhalperin.com writing
